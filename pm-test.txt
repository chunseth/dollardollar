# The Orange Ronin

At dawn, an orange buckles on a peel-bright blade,
Its citrus heart steady beneath the dew.
It walks the orchard rows without a master,
Guarding each blossom from the hungry blue.

When evening folds its purple flag of rain,
The ronin bows where fallen petals lie.
Its scent rises like a small, brave lantern,
And stars taste sweetness in the darkened sky.

---

# The Samurai of the Grove

Three oranges stand as samurai at noon,
Round helmets gleaming in the summer heat.
They serve the patient trees that made them golden,
With fragrant vows and roots beneath their feet.

Their katanas are leaves that turn the sunlight,
Their battle cry a burst of oil and flame.
They keep the grove in peace through winter's silence,
Until spring calls every blossom by its name.
